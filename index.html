A private desktop tool that reads product photos and details from local folders, creates Etsy listings, and publishes Pinterest Pins linking to those listings.

Read the privacy policy ↗

How the tool works

  1. Prepare the product.

    The operator supplies original images, titles, descriptions, prices, and other product information in local folders.

  2. Connect owned accounts.

    Account access uses the platforms' OAuth authorization flows. Pinterest and Etsy passwords are entered on their own websites.

  3. Validate and publish.

    The tool validates its inputs and uses the official APIs. It records completed steps to help resume interrupted work and avoid accidental repeat posts.

Built for personal business use

This tool is intended for accounts owned and managed by its operator. It is not a public service, account marketplace, or application available for other users to sign up to.

Publishing depends on platform approval and the permissions granted to each connected account. This website introduces the tool and its privacy practices; publishing runs on the operator's computer.
