Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Etsy Product Publisher handles information when its operator connects accounts and publishes product content, and how this informational website is used.

1. The tool and its operator

Etsy Product Publisher is a privately operated desktop publishing tool for the operator's own Etsy shops and Pinterest accounts. It is not offered as a service to other users. The operator can be contacted at anasbenhalima2003ben@gmail.com.

2. Information the tool uses

  • Product content: locally supplied images, digital product files where applicable, titles, descriptions, tags or keywords, prices, quantities, and listing details.
  • Connected account information: the configured account names, Etsy shop IDs and currency, Pinterest board and section IDs, and account or listing information returned by the platforms when verifying access and publishing.
  • Authorization credentials: app identifiers, app secrets, OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, expiry times, and granted scopes.
  • Operational records: file paths and content fingerprints, listing and Pin IDs and URLs, timestamps, publishing status, error summaries, local preview files, and run reports.

The tool does not request Pinterest or Etsy account passwords. Authentication takes place through the platform's own authorization pages. It does not use customer order, payment-card, or delivery-address information for this publishing workflow.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, verify the publishing destination, prepare and validate product content, upload listing assets, publish Etsy listings and Pinterest Pins, refresh authorization when supported, respect configured posting intervals and API rate limits, and track operations so interrupted runs can be resumed.

4. Storage and security

The desktop tool runs on the operator's computer. Product folders, previews, reports, and a publishing-state database are stored locally. When credentials are saved through the tool, app secrets and OAuth tokens are stored in the operating system's credential vault. Environment variables may also supply app credentials.

API communication uses HTTPS. The tool avoids recording API authorization headers and full API error-response bodies in its logs. Publicly published product content is stored by the receiving platform under that platform's own practices. The operator is responsible for protecting the computer, local files, backups, and credential store.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Product content and authorized API requests are sent to Pinterest or Etsy as needed to perform the requested publishing action. The tool does not sell account data, supply it to data brokers, or use it for third-party advertising.

This informational website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, such as IP addresses and request data, when serving the website. Contact emails are processed by the operator's email provider. These services and the publishing platforms have their own privacy policies:

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Etsy Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Retention and deletion

Product source files, publishing records, previews, and reports remain on the operator's computer until the operator removes them. Logs are rotated automatically, but local backups or copied reports may retain information separately. Saved authorization credentials remain in the operating system's credential vault until removed or replaced.

To stop account access, the operator can revoke the app's authorization through the relevant platform's account settings. Saved credentials can also be removed from the operating system's credential manager. Local records can be deleted from the computer and any backups. Deleting the publishing-state database removes duplicate-prevention history, so publishing should be stopped before those records are deleted.

Revoking access or deleting local files does not remove listings or Pins already published. Those must be managed through Etsy or Pinterest, and their retention practices continue to apply.

7. Website cookies and analytics

The supplied website contains no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded social-media widgets, account forms, or application cookies. It does not collect product files or OAuth credentials through a website form. The hosting provider may maintain its own technical request records.

8. Questions and requests

For questions about this tool, its authorization, or local data handling, contact anasbenhalima2003ben@gmail.com. Include enough detail to identify the request, but do not email passwords, app secrets, or access tokens.

9. Changes to this policy

The operator will update this page if the tool's data practices change. The date above identifies the most recent revision. If the tool is later offered to other users, its data handling and this policy must be reviewed before that service begins.
